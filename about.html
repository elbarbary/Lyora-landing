About us

Beauty booking, made honest.

LYORA is a booking app for Cairo’s makeup artists, hair stylists, nail and lash artists, and the salons they work in. Clients see real prices and real open times and book in a few taps. Artists get a booking link, one calendar and deposits that stop no-shows.

What we do

One place to book, and to be booked.

Built in Cairo for the way beauty is booked here — on Instagram, over WhatsApp, with a deposit on InstaPay.

For clients

Find an artist, see her prices and the times she’s actually free, and book without the back-and-forth. Free to use.

For artists

A booking link for your bio, one calendar for every booking, and deposits you confirm in a tap. No fee on your own clients.

For salons

Your whole team on one schedule, your own salon prices, and walk-ins added in seconds.

What we believe

Four rules we build by.

01

Prices up front.

Every service shows its price before you book. No “DM for price”, no surprises at the end.

02

Her clients are hers.

Anyone who books through an artist’s own link is her client. LYORA takes nothing on them, ever.

03

Money goes straight to the artist.

Deposits are sent to the artist’s own InstaPay address. LYORA never holds a client’s money.

04

Trust you can check.

Reviews come only from completed bookings, and the Verified badge means we checked her ID and portfolio on a call.

How we make money

Out in the open.

Clients pay LYORA nothing to book. Artists pay only when we bring them someone new.

0%

On an artist’s own clients

Bookings from her own link, plus walk-ins and DMs she adds herself.

15%

On a new client we bring

Only the first booking of a client who found her on LYORA. Once per client, billed monthly.

60 days

Free for founding partners

Artists and salons who join early pay no fees at all for their first 60 days.
