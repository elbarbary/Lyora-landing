FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is LYORA free for clients?

Yes. You pay the artist’s listed price and nothing to LYORA.

How do deposits work?

Some artists ask for a deposit to hold your slot. Once she confirms your booking, you send it to her own InstaPay address and add the transfer reference in the app. The money goes straight to her — LYORA never holds it.

What if I need to cancel?

Each artist sets a free-cancellation window — 12, 24 or 48 hours before — and you see it before you book. Cancel within it and your deposit is refunded. After it, her policy applies.

Where does LYORA work?

We’re starting in Cairo. Artists can come to you if they offer home visits — you’ll see it on their profile.

Is there an Android app?

It’s coming soon to Google Play. Leave your email below and we’ll tell you the day it’s out.

I’m an artist. What does LYORA cost?

Nothing for your own clients — anyone who books through your link, plus walk-ins and DMs you add yourself. LYORA charges 15% only on the first booking of a new client we bring you, once per client, billed monthly. Founding partners pay nothing for their first 60 days.
