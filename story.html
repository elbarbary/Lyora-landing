Our story

It started with “price?”

Booking a beauty artist in Cairo usually means a DM, a wait, a price, another wait, and a screenshot of a bank transfer. LYORA is our answer to all of it.

01The problem

Everyone was doing a booking system’s job by hand.

In Cairo, the best artists live on Instagram. Their work is there. Their prices and their calendars are not.

So a client sends “price?”, waits for a reply, asks whether Saturday is free, waits again, then sends a deposit to a phone number and a screenshot as proof. Multiply that by every bride, every graduation and every Thursday night.

On the other side, artists answer the same questions all day, keep bookings across DMs, WhatsApp and a notebook, and still lose whole afternoons to people who never show up.

Great artists shouldn’t have to be full-time receptionists.

02What we built

The step after Instagram, made effortless.

We didn’t try to replace how artists find clients — Instagram works. We wanted to fix what happens next.

Every artist on LYORA gets a link for her bio that shows her real prices and the times she’s actually free. Clients book themselves. Her deposit and cancellation policy is shown before anyone books, so nobody is surprised later.

Deposits go straight to the artist on InstaPay, with a transfer reference instead of a screenshot. Walk-ins, DMs and WhatsApp bookings go into the same calendar, so nothing is booked twice.

03How we earn

We only get paid when we bring her someone new.

It would have been easy to take a cut of every booking. We decided not to.

If a client found an artist through her own link, she’s the artist’s client, and LYORA takes nothing — not on the first booking, not ever. The same goes for every walk-in and DM she adds herself.

We charge 15% only on the first booking of a new client LYORA brought her, once per client. If we don’t grow her business, we don’t earn.

Her clients are her clients. That’s the deal.

04What’s next

Cairo first. Built with the artists who use it.

LYORA launches in Cairo soon, on iPhone and Android. Join the waitlist and we’ll tell you the day it’s live.

Our founding partners — the first artists and salons to join — pay nothing for their first 60 days, and we build the next version around what they tell us.

If you’re an artist, a salon, or someone who wants to help build this, write to us.
