Part A — Everyone

1. Who we are

LYORA (“we”, “us”) is a beauty-booking service operating in Egypt, based in Cairo. You can reach us at support@lyora.beauty.

2. What LYORA is

LYORA connects clients with independent beauty artists and salons. Artists and salons set their own services, prices, opening times and policies, and they provide the services themselves.

The agreement for a service is between you and the artist or salon you book. LYORA is not a party to it, does not employ artists, and does not provide beauty services.

3. Your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old to use LYORA.
  • Give accurate information and keep it up to date, including the phone number we use for your bookings.
  • Keep your sign-in secure. You are responsible for what happens on your account.
  • One person per account. Don’t create accounts for others or pretend to be someone else.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Security & sign-in → Delete account. Our Privacy Policy explains what happens to your data.

4. Bookings

  • Sending a booking request asks the artist or salon to reserve that time. The booking is confirmed when they accept it, or straight away if they have turned on automatic confirmation.
  • The service, price, duration, place and any travel fee for a home visit are shown before you send the request. The artist’s deposit and cancellation policy is shown too, and is saved with your booking.
  • Be on time, or at the address you gave for a home visit. Tell the artist in advance about anything that matters for the service, such as allergies or skin sensitivities.
  • Once a booking is confirmed, you and the artist can see each other’s phone number so you can call or message about it. Use it only for that booking.

5. Deposits and payment

  • An artist may ask for a deposit to hold your booking. You send it directly to the artist’s own InstaPay address shown in the app, then enter the transfer reference. The artist confirms when she receives it.
  • You pay the rest of the price directly to the artist or salon, as you agree with them.
  • LYORA does not receive, hold or transfer booking payments, and does not charge clients for booking.
  • Only send money to the artist named in your booking, to the address shown in the app. Never share your bank login details or one-time codes with anyone.

6. Cancellations, rescheduling and no-shows

  • Each artist sets a free-cancellation window (for example 12, 24 or 48 hours before the appointment) and decides what happens to a deposit after it. The policy saved with your booking is the one that applies.
  • If you cancel within the free-cancellation window, the artist refunds your deposit.
  • If you cancel after the window, the artist’s policy decides whether the deposit is refunded or kept.
  • If you don’t show up, the artist can mark the booking as a no-show and keep the deposit.
  • If the artist or salon cancels, they refund any deposit you paid in full.
  • Refunds are sent by the artist directly. If a refund you are owed doesn’t arrive, tell us at support@lyora.beauty. We will look into it and can restrict the artist’s account.

7. Reviews and reports

  • You can review an artist only after a completed booking with her.
  • Reviews must describe your own experience honestly. No insults, threats, discrimination, personal information or paid or fake reviews.
  • We may hide reviews that break these rules. A hidden review no longer counts towards the artist’s rating.
  • You can report a review or another user, and block someone, in the app. Our team reviews every report.

8. Subscriptions

LYORA may offer optional paid features, such as Style Match, as subscriptions bought through the App Store or Google Play.

  • The price and what is included are shown before you buy.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically every month until you cancel. Cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • A free trial may be offered once per person. If you don’t cancel before it ends, the paid subscription starts.
  • Payments and refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms.

Part B — Artists and salons

This part applies, in addition to Part A, if you offer services on LYORA as an artist or a salon (“you”).

B1. Your profile and listing

  • Keep your services, prices, durations, photos and opening times accurate and up to date. Only show work that is yours, with permission from anyone pictured.
  • Honour the bookings you confirm. Block time off in the app so clients can’t book it.
  • You work independently. You are responsible for your licences, taxes, products, hygiene and conduct. LYORA introduces clients to you. It does not employ you and does not direct how you provide your services.

B2. Your deposit and cancellation policy

  • You set your deposit and free-cancellation window in the app. Clients see the policy before they book, and the version in force is saved with each booking.
  • You must refund a client’s deposit in full if she cancels within your free-cancellation window, and whenever you cancel a booking.
  • You may keep a deposit after a late cancellation if your policy says so, and when you mark a client as a no-show.

B3. Receiving deposits

Clients send deposits to your own InstaPay address. Confirm a deposit in the app only after you have checked that it arrived. Don’t ask clients to pay any amount other than what the booking shows.

B4. Fees

  • No fee on clients who book through your own LYORA link, or on bookings you add yourself (walk-ins, DMs, WhatsApp and phone).
  • 15% of the service price (not including any travel fee) on the first completed booking of a new client who found you through LYORA. This is charged once per client. Later bookings from the same client are free.
  • Founding partners pay no fees for their first 60 days. Bookings in that period are shown on your statement as waived.
  • On the 1st of each month you receive a statement for the month before. Pay it within 14 days using the method shown on the statement.
  • If a statement is still unpaid after its due date, your profile is hidden from LYORA search until it is paid. Your booking link keeps working. Repeated non-payment can lead to suspension. Fees you owe remain payable if you close your account.

B5. Salons

A salon account is responsible for the artists it adds to its team, the salon prices it sets, and whether bookings are confirmed by hand or automatically. An artist joins a salon’s team only when both agree — the salon invites her and she accepts, or she asks and the salon accepts. Either side can end it in the app.

B6. Quality and reviews

To keep LYORA trustworthy, our team reviews profiles that receive credible complaints from several clients, or whose average rating falls below 3.8 after at least five reviews. A review can lead to a warning, the profile being hidden from search, or suspension. You can reply to us at support@lyora.beauty before we decide.

B7. Client information

Use clients’ names and phone numbers only to provide the booked service. Don’t add them to marketing lists or share them without their consent. You must follow Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020.
